Terms & Conditions

The terms that apply when you order AI-generated video advertisements from Grafiora.

Services

Grafiora produces AI-generated, UGC style video advertisements based on the brief and assets you provide. Packages are: Single (1 video, $29), Growth (5 videos, $119) and Scale (10 videos, $199). Every package includes captions and two revision rounds per video.

Ordering & payment

An order begins when we receive your completed brief and payment. Payments are accepted through PayPal, bank transfer and other payment methods agreed with you. Prices are in US dollars. Delivery timelines (1 day for Single, 2 days for Growth, 3 days for Scale) begin after we receive a complete brief and all required brand assets.

Your responsibilities

  • Provide accurate product information and any claims you want featured
  • Confirm you own or have the rights to use all media and assets you supply
  • Review delivered videos for compliance with your industry’s advertising rules before publishing

Revisions

Each video includes two revision rounds covering reasonable changes to pacing, captions, selected visuals and music within the approved creative direction. Requests that change the approved concept, product or script direction may be quoted as a new video.

Cancellations & refunds

You may cancel your order for a full refund at any time before production on your videos begins. Once production has begun, refunds are assessed case by case based on the work completed; contact us at grafiora.agency@gmail.com and we will review your order promptly and fairly. Delivered final videos that match the approved brief are not refundable solely because advertising performance varies, ad results depend on many factors outside creative, including targeting, budget and market conditions.

On full payment, you receive the right to use the delivered final videos in your own advertising and marketing, including paid social campaigns, without time limit. Grafiora retains the right to reuse general creative techniques and know-how. Raw project files, prompts and working materials are not included unless agreed in writing.

Grafiora’s videos are produced with AI generation tools. Where a platform or regulator requires disclosure of AI-generated or synthetic content, you are responsible for applying that disclosure when publishing the videos.
