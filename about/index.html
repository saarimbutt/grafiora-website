About Grafiora

Grafiora produces AI-generated, UGC style video advertisements based on the brief and assets you provide.

Grafiora creates conversion focused AI video ads for brands, startups and DTC businesses, without creator coordination, product shipping or costly traditional production. Every video arrives complete, with captions, voiceover, revisions and every format your placements need.

We create the hook, script, visuals, voiceover, captions and edit. You share your product, audience, offer, and references, review the first version, and receive platform-ready files for TikTok, Reels, Shorts and paid social.

Packages are: Single (1 video, $29), Growth (5 videos, $119) and Scale (10 videos, $199). Every package includes captions and two revision rounds per video.
