Privacy Policy

How Grafiora collects, uses and protects the information you share when ordering AI video ads.

Information we collect

When you place an order or contact us, we collect the details you provide in the order brief: your name, business email, brand or company name, website, product information, campaign goals and the brand assets you send (such as logos, product images, guidelines and footage). We do not ask for payment card details on this website; payments are accepted through PayPal, bank transfer and other payment methods agreed with you.

How we use your information

  • To produce, revise and deliver your video advertisements
  • To communicate about your order, revisions and delivery
  • To issue invoices and payment links
  • To improve our services and creative process

Your brand assets

Assets you provide are used only to produce your order. We do not sell your assets or use them for other clients. Finished videos may be shown in Grafiora’s portfolio only with your permission, unless the video has already been published publicly by you as an advertisement.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share information only with the service providers needed to run your order (for example, email, file delivery and payment processing) and only to the extent required.

Data retention & security

We keep order records and briefs for as long as needed to support revisions, accounting and legal obligations. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, though no online transmission is completely secure.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information at any time by emailing grafiora.agency@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available on this page.
